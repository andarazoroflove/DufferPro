### Highlights at a Glance
- **Pure ANSI C (C99):** Headless simulation, fixed-point deterministic physics, and software 2.5D rasterizer.
- **Three Environmental Themes:** Bushwood Country Club, La Playa Desert Festival, and Nocturnal Green Transylvania.
- **Multi-Platform Support:** Windows PC, macOS Sonoma (Universal 2 Apple Silicon / Intel), and real Game Boy Advance hardware.

### Versioning Protocol
- **Step Changes:** Increment `0.0.xx` on each step build (`0.7.5` $\to$ `0.7.6` $\to$ `0.7.7`...).
- **Milestone Rollover:** Increment middle step upon reaching `x.x.99` (`0.7.99` $\to$ `0.8.0`).
- **Published Releases:** Formal distribution releases are tagged and published strictly on ending multiples of 5 (e.g. `0.7.5`, `0.7.10`, `0.7.15`, ...).
